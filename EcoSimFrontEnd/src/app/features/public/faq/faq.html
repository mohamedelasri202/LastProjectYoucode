01 // How is "Road Incline" calculated?

The Intelligence Unit analyzes the roadInclineDegree from your Trip Profile. Gravity significantly alters fuel-to-weight ratios; our algorithms adjust the engine's load factor for every degree of elevation.

02 // City vs Highway Telemetry?

Urban environments involve frequent braking and idling (Kinetic Energy Recovery loss), while highways focus on aerodynamic drag. EcoSIM allows you to set a percentage split (e.g., 30/70) for mixed-use logistics routes.

03 // AI Advisory Generation?

Raw telemetry data is fed into our Neural Relay (Google Gemini). The AI compares your results against optimal efficiency benchmarks and generates a strategic brief on how to reduce your $CO_2$ footprint.

04 // Multiple Fuel Types?

Affirmative. You can register Petrol, Diesel, or Electric units. By running the same Mission Profile across different chassis, you can directly compare the "Ecological Cost" of each engine type.
